About Us

The Pulse of Lobatse, Broadcasting to the World.

Bringing local culture, breaking music, and community voices to your screens and speakers, wherever you are.

Lobatse Online Radio is a digital broadcasting platform dedicated to amplifying the voice of our community. We bridge the gap between traditional radio and modern streaming, proving that great local storytelling and music can travel across borders.

What We Do

  • Champion Local Talent: We are an incubator for rising broadcasters, DJs, and artists. From Afropop to underground beats, we give creators the platform they deserve.
  • Connect the Community: We go beyond music. LOR is a hub for local stories, cultural pride, and conversations that matter to the people of Lobatse and our diaspora.
  • Global Reach, Local Heart: Through accessible online streaming, our listeners can tune in from a smartphone or computer anywhere in the world, staying connected to home.

Partner With Us

Reach a highly engaged, loyal audience that supports local. We offer accessible, modern advertising and sponsorship packages for businesses looking to grow their brand alongside our community.
